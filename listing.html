SF Cable's Cat5e Ethernet Patch Cables are rated for 350MHz bandwidth and support Gigabit Ethernet (1000BASE-T) for fast, reliable network connections. Built with 24AWG bare copper conductors and precision-matched impedance, they deliver consistent performance in home networks, office infrastructure, data centers, and AV installations.

Available in UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair), STP (Shielded Twisted Pair), and Plenum-rated versions, our Cat5e cables meet or exceed TIA/EIA-568-B.2 specifications. All cables feature 50-micron gold-plated RJ-45 connectors and are 100% tested before shipping.

With over 20 years of industry experience, SF Cable offers Cat5e patch cables in lengths from 1 ft to 100 ft and six colors — Blue, Black, White, Gray, Red, and Yellow — so you can color-code your network at a glance. Every cable is backed by our Lifetime Warranty. Orders over $75 ship free, and in-stock items are available for same-day dispatch.
